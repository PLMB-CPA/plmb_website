About the Firm

Po Lao Marquez Benedictos & Co. is a Philippine accounting firm established in 1984 in Quezon City. We provide audit, tax, and advisory services to a wide range of clients — from publicly listed corporations to small and medium enterprises navigating their first statutory audit.

Our Mission

We aim to safeguard corporate health by delivering premium independent audit, tax, accounting, and management consultancy services. We add distinct value by critically evaluating and optimizing risk management, internal compliance, and governance frameworks.
