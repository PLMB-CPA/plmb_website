Our Services

Audit and Assurance

We deliver independent, objective assurance services designed to enhance the credibility of your financial statements and strengthen your organizational governance.

  • Statutory Financial Audits
    Comprehensive examination of financial records to ensure full compliance with the Philippine Financial Reporting Standards (PFRS).
  • Technical Standards & Quality Control
    Rigorous internal reviews led by our quality control specialists to ensure every audit report seamlessly passes regulatory scrutiny.
  • Internal Control Evaluation
    Critical assessment of your existing internal workflows to detect compliance gaps, prevent fraud, and mitigate operational risks.

Tax Advisory and Compliance

We help organizations navigate the complex and ever-changing Philippine tax landscape while legally optimizing their tax exposure.

  • Corporate Tax Compliance
    Seamless preparation, review, and electronic filing of monthly, quarterly, and annual BIR tax returns.
  • Tax Advisory & Planning
    Strategic structuring of commercial transactions to minimize tax liabilities and ensure alignment with recent tax reforms.
  • BIR Audit Defense & Assessment
    Expert technical assistance and representation during Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) letter of authority (LOA) audits.

Management Consultancy

We look beyond traditional historical numbers to turn your financial data into a forward-looking management information resource.

  • Business Process Optimization
    Designing robust risk management frameworks and internal controls tailored to your specific industry dynamics.
  • Financial Performance Analysis
    In-depth review of financial health to help management make data-driven, strategic growth decisions.
  • Corporate Secretarial & Compliance
    Assistance with SEC reporting, local business permit renewals, and other statutory corporate maintenance.
